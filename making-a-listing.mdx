Choose **Start a listing** (or **New listing**) to begin. Parcelona asks a few quick questions, one screen at a time.

## Adding photos

Every listing needs at least one photo, and the first one is the cover. Choose **Take photos** to use the camera, or **Choose from library** to pick photos you already have. Front, back, any marks, and the label make for the best drafts. You can change the order and pick a different cover after adding them.

## Describing the item

Next, give the item a name. This part is optional, since Claude can usually tell what something is from the photos, but a brand and model number help it get things right. Then pick a condition, from new to for parts, and add anything a buyer should know about, like scratches, missing pieces, or what comes in the box.

Last, choose where it should go: eBay, your Facebook group, or both.

## Getting a draft

Choose **Research and draft** and Parcelona gets to work. Claude looks at your answers and the first few photos, searches the web for what similar items have sold for, and writes a price, a title, and a description for each place you're posting. If it needs to know something it can't figure out, like which model you have, it asks you a quick question. You can answer, or choose **Draft anyway** to let it make its best guess.

You can choose how Claude prices things in **Accounts › Pricing and instructions**. *Gone by Friday* prices to sell quickly, *Going rate* aims for a fair price, and *Worth the wait* prices near the top for a buyer who wants that exact item. You can also give it instructions to follow on every listing, such as a tone you like or something you always want mentioned.

## Reviewing and posting

When the draft is ready you can change anything on it, including the price, the title, and the descriptions. When it looks right, post it, or choose **Save as draft** to come back later. Your listings and their photos are saved on your phone.

Posting keeps going if you close the app, and Parcelona shows how it went when you come back. If a post fails, choose **Try again** once you've fixed the problem.
