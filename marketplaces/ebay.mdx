Parcelona posts fixed-price eBay listings using your own business policies. Listings stay up until they sell or you end them on eBay.

## Connect eBay

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open eBay settings">
    Open **Settings** (⌘,) and go to **eBay**. You can also connect from the last setup screen when you first open Parcelona.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Connect eBay">
    Choose **Connect eBay…**. eBay's own sign-in page opens in your browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve Parcelona">
    Sign in to eBay and approve Parcelona. Settings updates by itself once you're connected.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Parcelona keeps a token that lasts 30 days and renews each time it posts, so you stay connected as long as you keep listing. The token is stored in your Mac's Keychain. Parcelona's server never stores your eBay account or listings.

To disconnect, choose **Disconnect** in **Settings › eBay**.

## Business policies

eBay needs a shipping, payment, and return policy, and a location to ship from, on every listing. Parcelona uses the business policies already on your eBay account.

1. If Settings shows **Your account needs business policies turned on before it can list**, choose **Turn on**.
2. Under **Business policies**, pick your **Shipping**, **Payment**, and **Returns** policies, and where you **Ship from**.
3. If there are no locations yet, enter your ZIP code and choose **Add**.

If you add or change policies on eBay, choose **Reload from eBay** to see them in Parcelona.

<Tip>
  Create or edit your policies in eBay under **Account settings › Business policies**. Then choose **Reload from eBay**.
</Tip>

## Offers

Turn on **Allow Best Offer** to let buyers send offers. Then set:

- **Accept at**: offers at or above this percent of your price are accepted automatically.
- **Decline at**: offers at or below this percent are declined automatically.

Offers in between come to you on eBay to answer.

## Item details

Claude picks the eBay category and fills in the item details eBay requires for it, such as brand and model. Under **Item details**, add anything you want it to always do. See [Customize drafts](/listings/customize-drafts).

## Pick eBay by default

**Pick eBay on new listings** checks eBay on the **Where** step of every new listing. You can still uncheck it per listing.

## What gets posted

When you post, Parcelona uploads your photos to eBay (HEIC photos are turned into JPEG first), creates the item, and publishes the listing. When it's done, the review screen shows **Live on eBay** with a link to the listing.
