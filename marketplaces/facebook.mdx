Parcelona posts to a Facebook buy and sell group you choose, with the cover photo.

<Info>
  Facebook needs Google Chrome installed on your Mac. Parcelona opens Chrome to sign in and to post.
</Info>

## Sign in to Facebook

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Facebook settings">
    Open **Settings** (⌘,) and go to **Facebook**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Sign in">
    Parcelona opens a Chrome window with Facebook's sign-in page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in yourself">
    Sign in as you normally would, including any security checks Facebook asks for. The window closes by itself once you reach your Facebook home page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Settings then shows the email of the Facebook account you signed in with.

Facebook sign-in and posting happen entirely on your Mac. Your Facebook password, cookies and posts never reach Parcelona's server.

## Choose your group

Under **Group**, enter:

- **Name**: the group's name, for example *Arlington Buy & Sell*.
- **Link**: the group's address, like `https://www.facebook.com/groups/…`. Copy it from your browser while you're on the group's page.

Choose **Open the group** to check the link. You need to be a member of the group to post in it.

## Pickup and delivery

- **Meetup**: where and when buyers can pick up, for example *Ballston Metro, evenings*. It goes into the post through the `{pickup}` placeholder.
- **Offer shipping too**: say in the post that you can ship.

## Instructions and format

Group posts read better short and friendly. Use **Instructions** and **Description format** to shape them. See [Customize drafts](/listings/customize-drafts).

## Pick Facebook by default

**Pick Facebook on new listings** checks Facebook on the **Where** step of every new listing.

## After you post

- **Live in your Facebook group** means the post is up, with a link to it.
- **Submitted to the group. Waiting for admin approval.** means the group's admins review posts first. It goes live once they approve it.
- If Facebook didn't confirm the post, Parcelona says so. Check the group before you choose **Retry**, so you don't post twice.
