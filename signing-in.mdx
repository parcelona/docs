Parcelona uses your Google account. Your account is what counts your [free listings](/account/free-plan) and lets Parcelona draft and post.

## Sign in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Continue with Google">
    On the first screen of Parcelona, choose **Continue with Google**, or press **↵**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your account in the browser">
    Your browser opens Google's sign-in page. Pick your Google account there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go back to Parcelona">
    Parcelona updates by itself once you're signed in. You don't need to copy a code or close anything.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Parcelona keeps your sign-in in your Mac's Keychain, so you stay signed in after you quit.

<Note>
  If the browser page sits for a long time, Parcelona stops waiting and shows **Sign-in took too long. Try again.** Choose **Continue with Google** again.
</Note>

## Sign out

Open **Settings** (⌘,), go to **General**, and choose **Sign Out** under **Account**.

Signing out doesn't delete anything. Your listings and photos stay on your Mac, and you can sign in again at any time. To remove your account from Parcelona's server, see [Delete your account](/account/privacy-and-deletion#delete-your-account).

## If you're asked to sign in again

If your session ends, Parcelona shows **Your session ended. Sign in again.** or **Sign in to Parcelona again to post.** Sign in the same way as above. Your open listings stay where they were.
