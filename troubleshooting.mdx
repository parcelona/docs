## Signing in

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Sign-in took too long. Try again.">
    Parcelona stopped waiting for Google. Choose **Continue with Google** again and finish picking your account in the browser. If the browser didn't open, check that you have a default browser set in **System Settings › Desktop & Dock**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Your session ended. Sign in again.">
    Your Parcelona sign-in expired or was signed out elsewhere. Sign in again with Google. Your open listings stay where they were.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can't reach the server. Trying again">
    Parcelona can't reach its server, usually because your Mac is offline. It keeps trying by itself. Check your internet connection, and any VPN or firewall that might block Parcelona.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Drafting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The draft looks generic or the price is off">
    If Parcelona couldn't reach its server, or the reply didn't come back complete, you get a simple stand-in draft. Check your connection, press **⌘[** to go back to your answers, then **↵** to draft again.

    To get a better draft, add the brand and exact model to the name, add notes about condition, and put your clearest photo first. Claude sees the first four photos.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The connection to Parcelona dropped before the listing came back.">
    The connection broke while Claude was drafting. Press **↵** to draft again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Add at least one photo to continue">
    Every listing needs a photo. See [Add photos](/listings/photos-and-drafting#add-photos).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My phone photos aren't showing up">
    Make sure the listing is on its **Photos** step. Photos that arrive while no listing is on that step wait for the next one. If the QR code page on your phone stopped working, quit and reopen Parcelona, then choose **Use phone** and scan again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="AirDrop photos aren't showing up">
    Parcelona watches your **Downloads** folder while a listing is on its **Photos** step. Check that AirDrop saved the photos to **Downloads**, and that the listing was on the Photos step when they arrived. You can also drag them from **Downloads** onto the window.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Posting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Connect eBay or Facebook in Settings (⌘,) to post this.">
    You haven't connected a marketplace yet. See [Post to eBay](/marketplaces/ebay) or [Post to Facebook](/marketplaces/facebook). You can still save the listing as a draft with **⌘S**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Your account needs business policies turned on before it can list.">
    eBay requires business policies. In **Settings › eBay**, choose **Turn on**, then pick your policies. See [Business policies](/marketplaces/ebay#business-policies).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Pick a shipping, payment, and return policy, and a location in Settings › eBay.">
    Every eBay listing needs all four. Pick them under **Business policies** in **Settings › eBay**. If a list is empty, create the policy on eBay, then choose **Reload from eBay**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Add the weight with packing. eBay needs it for calculated shipping.">
    Your eBay shipping policy uses calculated shipping, so eBay needs the package weight. On the review screen, enter the weight of the item packed for shipping, then post again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Sign in to eBay in Settings first.">
    Your eBay connection ended, or you haven't connected yet. Choose **Connect eBay…** in **Settings › eBay**. The connection lasts 30 days and renews each time you post.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Sign in to Facebook in Settings first.">
    Choose **Sign in** in **Settings › Facebook** and sign in in the Chrome window. Make sure Google Chrome is installed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Set the Facebook group link in Settings first.">
    Parcelona needs to know which group to post in. Paste the group's link under **Group** in **Settings › Facebook**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Facebook did not confirm a published post. Check the group before retrying.">
    The post may have gone through even though Facebook didn't confirm it. Open the group and look for it before you choose **Retry**, so you don't post twice.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Submitted to the group. Waiting for admin approval.">
    Nothing is wrong. Your group's admins review posts first, and it goes live once they approve it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Your account and the app

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="You've used every free listing.">
    You've posted all 25 free listings. Email [hello@parcelona.app](mailto:hello@parcelona.app) and we'll get you more. See [Free plan](/account/free-plan).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Parcelona couldn't be reached to count this listing.">
    Parcelona checks with its server before posting so the free plan can count the listing. Check your connection and post again. Nothing was posted.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Parcelona needs an update">
    This version is too old to draft or post. Choose **Update** to install the latest version. Parcelona also updates itself when you quit.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
