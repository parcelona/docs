Open **Settings** (⌘,) to shape how Parcelona writes every listing.

## Pricing

In **Settings › Agent › Pricing**, choose how fast you want things gone. Claude weighs recent sales, condition, demand and competing listings, then leans the price this way.

| Pricing | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Gone by Friday** | Price to sell this week, even if it leaves a little on the table. |
| **Going rate** | A fair price for what it is. Sells at a normal pace. |
| **Worth the wait** | Price near the top and wait for the buyer who wants this exact one. |

## Instructions for every listing

In **Settings › Agent**, add instructions Claude follows on every listing: tone, what to always mention, what to never say. The **Brief** section previews what Claude gets to work with.

Each marketplace has its own instructions too:

- **Settings › eBay › Item details.** Claude picks the eBay category and fills in the details eBay requires. Add anything you want it to always do, such as "Use the exact model number in the title."
- **Settings › Facebook › Instructions.** Group posts read better short and friendly, for example "Keep it under 80 words. Put the price in the first line."

## Description format

Each marketplace's settings have an optional **Description format**: a template Claude writes to. Use these placeholders in it:

| Placeholder | Filled with |
| --- | --- |
| `{title}` | The listing title |
| `{price}` | The price, filled in when you post |
| `{quantity}` | How many are available, filled in when you post |
| `{condition}` | The condition you picked |
| `{notes}` | Your notes for a buyer |
| `{shipping}` | Your shipping details |
| `{returns}` | Your return policy |
| `{pickup}` | Your Facebook meetup spot |

For example, a Facebook format:

```text Facebook description format
{title}, {condition}
{notes}

Pickup: {pickup}
```
