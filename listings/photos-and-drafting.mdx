## Add photos

Every listing needs at least one photo. The first photo is the cover, and you can drag photos to reorder them. Use any of these on the **Photos** step:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Your phone">
    Choose **Use phone**. Parcelona shows a QR code. Scan it with your phone's camera to open a camera page, then take photos. Each photo goes straight into the listing on your Mac.

    You only scan once. The link stays open until you quit Parcelona, so the next listing can use the same phone. Photos you take while no listing is on the Photos step wait for the next one.

    Each photo is encrypted on your phone with a key only your Mac has. Parcelona's server passes it along and keeps nothing.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="AirDrop">
    AirDrop photos to your Mac while you're on the Photos step. Parcelona watches your **Downloads** folder, where AirDrop saves them, and adds new photos to the listing. The step shows **Listening for AirDrop** while it watches.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Drag and drop">
    Drag photos from Finder or another app onto the window, or use the file picker to choose them.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### Crop and flip

Point at a photo and choose the crop button to crop it, freeform or square, or flip it horizontally or vertically. You can also crop the cover on the review screen.

Parcelona saves an edited copy, and that copy is what Claude sees and what gets posted. Your original photo is never changed, and **Reset** puts it back.

## How drafting works

Press **↵** on the last step to start. Parcelona sends what you told it about the item (its name, condition and notes, your instructions and pricing choice) and up to the first four photos to Parcelona's server, where Claude:

1. Works out what the item is, from your name or from the photos.
2. Searches the web for what it sells for, including recent sold prices.
3. Writes a price, a title, and a description for each marketplace you picked.

While it works you see a live log of its searches.

The price leans the way you set in **Settings › Agent › Pricing**: *Gone by Friday*, *Going rate*, or *Worth the wait*. See [Customize drafts](/listings/customize-drafts).

## When Claude has a question

Sometimes Claude can't tell something it needs, such as which model you have. It stops and shows **Claude has a question** with a few answers to pick from.

- Pick an answer, or type your own and press **↵**.
- Press **⌘D** (**Draft anyway**) to skip the question. Claude uses its best guess and doesn't ask again for that draft.

## If drafting can't finish

If Parcelona can't reach its server, or the reply doesn't come back complete, you get a simple stand-in draft instead so you can still edit and save the listing. Press **⌘[** to go back to your answers, then **↵** to draft again. See [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting#drafting).
