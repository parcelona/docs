## Review the draft

After drafting, Parcelona shows the review screen. Everything on it is editable:

- **Price.** What Claude suggests, from what similar items sell for and the pricing you chose.
- **Title.** What buyers see first in search and in the group.
- **Description.** One for each marketplace you picked. Facebook posts are shorter and friendlier than eBay listings.
- **Photos.** Drag to reorder. The first one is the cover.

To change your answers (name, condition, notes, photos or where), press **⌘[** for **Edit answers**, then draft again.

## Post or save

| Keys | Action |
| --- | --- |
| **⌘↵** | Post to every marketplace you picked |
| **⌘S** | Save as a draft without posting |

Posting and saving both write the listing to your listings folder on your Mac: a folder per listing with `listing.json` and its photos. The folder is `~/Parcelona/listings` unless you change it in **Settings › General**.

After posting, each marketplace shows how it went:

- **Live on eBay**, with a link to the listing.
- **Live in your Facebook group**, with a link to the post.
- **Submitted**, when your Facebook group needs an admin to approve posts.
- **Not posted**, with what went wrong. Fix it, then choose **Retry** next to that marketplace. The other marketplaces stay as they are.

<Note>
  A listing counts toward your [free plan](/account/free-plan) once, when it first posts. Posting to eBay and Facebook together counts once. Saving a draft never counts.
</Note>

## The archive

A listing that posted moves to the archive when you leave it: when you switch tabs, open the archive, or quit. A listing that failed to post stays open so you can fix it.

- **⌘E** archives any listing.
- **⇧⌘E**, or the box button next to **New listing**, shows the archive, newest first.
- **Restore** opens an archived listing again on its review screen.

## Open listings are kept

Listings you're working on are still there when you quit and reopen Parcelona. A listing that was drafting opens on its last step, so press **↵** to draft again. Listings you close, and listings that posted, aren't kept as open tabs.
