Parcelona is a Mac app for selling the things you don't need anymore. You say what the item is, what shape it's in, and where it should go, then add a few photos. Parcelona looks up what it sells for and writes the title, the price and a description for each marketplace. You check it over, then post.

## How a listing works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe it">
    Give the item a name (optional, Parcelona can tell from the photos), pick its condition, and add notes for a buyer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add photos">
    Take them with your phone, AirDrop them, or drop them on the window. The first photo is the cover.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick where to sell">
    eBay, your Facebook group, or both.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let Parcelona research it">
    Claude searches the web for what the item sells for and drafts the listing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and post">
    Edit the price, title or descriptions, then post, or save it as a draft.
  </Step>
</Steps>
