Parcelona is an iPhone app that helps you sell things you don't need anymore. You take a few photos, say what the item is and what condition it's in, and it looks up what the item sells for and writes the listing for you. When you're happy with it, Parcelona posts it to eBay, to a Facebook buy and sell group, or to both.

## What you need

You'll need an iPhone running iOS 18 or later, and a Google account to sign in with. To post anywhere you'll also need an eBay account, a Facebook account, or both, though you can draft listings without either.

## Signing in

The first time you open Parcelona, choose **Continue with Google** and pick your Google account. You'll then see a quick note about the [free plan](/account) and a screen where you can connect eBay or Facebook. You can do that now or later from **Accounts** (the person button at the top of your listings), as described in [Posting to eBay and Facebook](/posting).

When you're ready, choose **Start a listing** and follow [Making a listing](/making-a-listing).

If you have questions or something isn't working, email us at [hello@parcelona.app](mailto:hello@parcelona.app).
