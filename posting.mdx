You can connect eBay, Facebook, or both from **Accounts**, the person button at the top of your listings. You still choose where each listing goes when you make it.

## eBay

To connect eBay, open **Accounts › eBay** and choose **Connect eBay**. eBay's own sign-in page opens in the app, where you sign in and approve Parcelona. Your connection lasts 30 days and renews every time you post, so in practice you stay connected as long as you keep listing.

eBay needs every listing to have a shipping policy, a payment policy, a return policy, and a ZIP code to ship from. Parcelona uses the business policies already set up on your eBay account. If your account doesn't have them yet, choose **Set up** and Parcelona makes simple ones for you. If you add or change policies on eBay later, choose **Reload from eBay** so Parcelona sees them.

You can also turn on **Allow Best Offer** and set percentages for offers to accept or decline automatically. Listings are fixed price and stay up until they sell or you end them on eBay.

## Facebook

Parcelona posts to a Facebook buy and sell group of your choosing, using the listing's cover photo.

To sign in, open **Accounts › Facebook** and choose **Sign in to Facebook**. Facebook's login page opens in the app, running in a secure browser in the cloud, and you sign in the way you normally would. What you type goes through Parcelona, which doesn't keep it. The page closes on its own once you reach your Facebook home page, and you stay signed in for later posts. **Sign out of Facebook** removes that sign-in.

Next, paste your group's link, which you can copy from the Facebook app or your browser while you're on the group's page. You can also add a usual meetup spot for pickups and say whether you're happy to ship.

Some groups have admins approve every post. If yours does, Parcelona shows the post as submitted, and it goes live once an admin approves it. If Parcelona says Facebook didn't confirm a post, check the group before trying again so you don't end up posting twice.
