Parcelona is free while we're getting started. Your account can list **25 items**.

## What counts

- A listing counts **once, the first time it posts**.
- Posting a listing to eBay and Facebook at the same time counts once.
- **Drafts are free.** Drafting, editing, and saving as a draft never count.

## See how many are left

Open **Settings** (⌘,) and go to **General**. It shows how many listings you've used and how many are left. The account menu in the sidebar shows the same count.

## When you've used them all

Once you've posted 25 listings, Parcelona stops new posts and says you've used every free listing. You can still draft and save listings.

Need more? Choose **Ask for more** in **Settings › General**, or **Ask for More Listings…** in the sidebar's account menu, or email [hello@parcelona.app](mailto:hello@parcelona.app), and we'll get you more.
