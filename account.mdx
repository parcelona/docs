## The free plan

Parcelona is free while we're getting started, and every account can post 25 listings. A listing counts once, the first time it posts, even if you post it to eBay and Facebook at the same time. Drafting and saving drafts never count. You can see how many listings you have left in **Settings › General**, and if you run out, email [hello@parcelona.app](mailto:hello@parcelona.app) and we'll get you more.

## What Parcelona keeps

Most of what you do stays on your Mac, including your listings, photos, and settings. Your sign-ins for Parcelona, eBay, and Facebook are kept in your Mac's Keychain.

Our server keeps your Google name and email so you can sign in, along with a simple record of each listing you post so the free plan can count them. When you draft a listing, your answers and a few photos are sent to Claude to write it, and neither the photos nor the draft are saved. Photos from your phone are encrypted with a key only your Mac has, so our server just passes them along. We don't sell your information or show ads. The [privacy policy](https://parcelona.app/privacy) has the full details.

## Signing out and deleting your account

You can sign out from **Settings › General** at any time, and your listings stay on your Mac.

To delete your account, open **Settings › General**, choose **Delete account…**, and confirm. This removes your account and listing count from our server right away and signs you out. It doesn't take down anything you've already posted on eBay or Facebook, so end those listings there if you need to. If you'd rather, email [hello@parcelona.app](mailto:hello@parcelona.app) and we'll delete it for you.

To remove everything from your Mac as well, delete Parcelona from Applications, along with your listings folder and `~/Library/Application Support/Parcelona`.
