## The free plan

Parcelona is free while we're getting started, and every account can post 25 listings. A listing counts once, the first time it posts, even if you post it to eBay and Facebook at the same time. Drafting and saving drafts never count. You can see how many listings you have left in **Accounts**, and if you run out, email [hello@parcelona.app](mailto:hello@parcelona.app) and we'll get you more.

## What Parcelona keeps

Your listings, photos, and settings are saved on your phone. Your Parcelona and eBay sign-ins are kept in your iPhone's Keychain.

Our server keeps your Google name and email so you can sign in, along with a simple record of each listing you post so the free plan can count them. When you draft a listing, your answers and a few photos are sent to Claude to write it, and neither the photos nor the draft are saved. When you post, the listing and its photos go through our server to eBay or Facebook, and the photos are deleted once the post is done. Your Facebook sign-in is kept in a cloud browser profile that only your account uses. We don't sell your information or show ads. The [privacy policy](https://parcelona.app/privacy) has the full details.

## Signing out and deleting your account

You can sign out from **Accounts › Parcelona account** at any time, and your listings stay on your phone.

To delete your account, open **Accounts › Parcelona account**, choose **Delete account**, and confirm. This removes your account, listing count, and Facebook sign-in from our server right away and signs you out. It doesn't take down anything you've already posted on eBay or Facebook, so end those listings there if you need to. If you'd rather, email [hello@parcelona.app](mailto:hello@parcelona.app) and we'll delete it for you.

To remove everything from your phone as well, delete the Parcelona app.
