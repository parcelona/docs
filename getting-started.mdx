## What you need

- A Mac with Apple silicon, running macOS 14 or later.
- A Google account, to sign in to Parcelona.
- An eBay account, a Facebook account, or both, when you're ready to post. You can draft listings without either.
- Google Chrome, if you post to Facebook. Parcelona uses it to sign in to Facebook.

## Install

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download">
    Download [Parcelona.dmg](https://downloads.parcelona.app/Parcelona.dmg) from [parcelona.app](https://parcelona.app).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move it to Applications">
    Open the DMG and drag **Parcelona** to **Applications**. The app is notarized by Apple, so it opens without a warning.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Parcelona">
    The first time it opens, Parcelona walks you through three screens: [signing in with Google](/signing-in), the [free plan](/account/free-plan), and where you sell.
  </Step>
</Steps>

On the last setup screen you can connect eBay or Facebook right away, or skip it and connect them later in **Settings** (⌘,). See [eBay](/marketplaces/ebay) and [Facebook](/marketplaces/facebook).

## Make your first listing

Press **⌘T** for a new listing. Each listing is a few short steps, and **↵** moves you to the next one:

1. **Name.** What the item is. This is optional: brand and model help, but Claude can also tell from the photos.
2. **Condition.** New, Like new, Good, Fair, or For parts.
3. **Notes.** Missing parts, marks, what comes in the box. Optional.
4. **Photos.** At least one. See [Photos and drafting](/listings/photos-and-drafting).
5. **Where.** eBay, Facebook, or both.

Press **↵** on the last step and Parcelona researches the item and drafts the listing. Then you [review it and post](/listings/review-and-post).

## Updates

Parcelona updates itself. It checks for a new version every few hours and installs it the next time you quit the app.
